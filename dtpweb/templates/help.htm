dtp-web, the Roller Brake Test Search ... thing

Main Menu

This is a web-based tool for digging through the UK Department for Transport's roller brake-tester database, in order to show brake test information for a given test procedure number (DTp number, given on a VTG-6 or brake test result sheet), or by using the advanced search tool to look up an appropriate DTp number where none is available, or where the given DTp number is wrong. This happens more often than you would like to believe.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. ...why

    Because I needed to figure out what the appropriate DTp number would be for my truck restoration project, and there didn't appear to be a good way to actually find that out because nobody I've talked to who has an RL has managed to show a DTp number for it; mostly because the places that have tested their trucks, have used the brake tester in manual mode rather than VOSA mode, I think.

  2. Why is the background wood?

    Because it made the results cards look really funny. Which is also why they're lined paper.

The UK Department for Transport

The contents of the DTA database are a product of the UK Department for Transport, and are public sector information, updated versions of which are released via data.gov.uk sporadically. The latest version (updated 6/7/2023) was released on 24 Nov 2025 due to a FOIA request, and is listed as "License not specified".
