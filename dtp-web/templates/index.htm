dtp-web Roller Brake Test Search

This is a tool for digging through the Department for Transport's roller brake test database, in order to find a brake test procedure by DTP number, or by using an advanced search in order to look up an appropriate DTP number where none is easily available.

Truck Search

Search for trucks -- rigids or tractor units -- by the DTP on their VTG 6 plate, or by advanced search.

Trailer Search

Search for trailers -- drawbar or semi-articulated -- by DTP on the plate, or by advanced search.

PSV Search

Search for buses and coaches by their DTP, or by advanced search.
